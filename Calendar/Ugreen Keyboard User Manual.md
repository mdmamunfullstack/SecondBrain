#### Bluetooth Connection (Channels B1, B2)

1. On the keyboard, press the **Fn** key simultaneously with either the **B1** or **B2** key to select a Bluetooth channel. The corresponding indicator light will flash slowly.
2. To enter pairing mode, press and hold the **Fn** key and the selected Bluetooth channel key (**B1** or **B2**) for 3-5 seconds until the indicator light flashes rapidly.
3. On your device (computer, tablet, smartphone), enable Bluetooth and search for new devices.
4. Select "UGREEN K551" from the list of available devices.
5. Once paired, the indicator light on the keyboard will stop flashing and remain solid for a few seconds before turning off.

### Switching Between Connected Devices

The keyboard allows seamless switching between up to three connected devices (one 2.4G and two Bluetooth devices).

- To switch to the 2.4G connected device, press the **Fn** key and the **2.4G** key (often labeled with a USB icon or '1').
- To switch to a Bluetooth connected device, press the **Fn** key and the corresponding Bluetooth channel key (**B1** or **B2**).       

Common Shortcuts
