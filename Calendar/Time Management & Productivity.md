


## Here are some productivity tips

1. Sound Sleep
2. Avoid electric gadgets at least 45 minutes before sleep
3. Strive to be the best version of yourself
4. Focus on completing one task at a time
5. Avoid multitasking
6. Start with small steps
7. Utilize free time effectively
8. Remember the 4D approach (Delete, Delay, Delegate, Do)
9. Aim for Win-Win solutions
10. Limit social media usage to 30 minutes
11. Start each day with a plan
12. Try the Pomodoro Technique for productivity
13. Create a 'not-to-do' list
14. Consider time in minutes rather than hours
15. Get started on your tasks
16. Minimize distractions
17. Keep your workplace clean and organized
18. Prepare your work plan for the next day
19. Avoid saying "I don't have time."
20. Try the 2-5-20 time management method
21. Block out specific time for tasks
22. Strive for work-life balance (note: Balance is a myth)
23. Follow the process of Build, Measure, Learn
24. Apply the Learn, Apply, Share approach to advance your career
25. Use a notebook for taking notes
26. Utilize a calendar or to-do list to block out time
